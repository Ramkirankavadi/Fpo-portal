What Is a CBBO & What Is Its 5-Year Role?

Empanelled by Implementing Agencies (SFAC, NABARD, NCDC, NAFED, etc.), CBBOs bring multidisciplinary teams across agronomy, agribusiness, accounting, IT/MIS, and social mobilization.

1

Cluster Identification & Baseline

Conduct diagnostic baseline surveys, map One-District-One-Product / commodity clusters, and identify viable blocks for FPO formation.

2

Farmer Mobilization & Registration

Form Farmer Interest Groups (FIGs), onboard minimum 300 members (100 in NE/Hilly areas), and incorporate under Companies Act or Cooperative Societies Act.

3

Governance, BoD & CEO Training

Recruit FPO CEOs and accountants, train Board of Directors (including mandatory women directors), and enroll members on the NeGD/SIDH LMS portal.

4

Business Plan & Statutory Licenses

Prepare 5-year viable business plans and secure Mandi, Seed, Fertilizer, Pesticide, FSSAI, and GST registrations for input & output trade.

5

Equity Grant & Credit Facilitation

Prepare applications for Matching Equity Grant (up to â‚¹15 lakh) and bank project loans backed by Credit Guarantee Cover (up to â‚¹2 crore).

6

Digital & B2B Market Linkages

Onboard FPOs onto e-NAM, ONDC Mystore, and GeM, arrange B2B corporate buyers, and facilitate participation in SFAC FPO Melas.
