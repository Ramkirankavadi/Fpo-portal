Operational & Incubation Framework

What Is a CBBO & What Is Its 5-Year Role?

Empanelled by Implementing Agencies (SFAC, NABARD, NCDC, NAFED, FDRVC, NDDB, NERAMAC, TRIFED & State IAs), CBBOs field 5-domain specialist teams (Agronomy, Agri-Marketing, Value Addition, Social Mobilization & Accounting/IT) with up to ₹25 lakh/FPO support over 5 years.

1

Cluster Identification & Baseline

Conduct diagnostic baseline surveys, map commodity/ODOP clusters, mobilize farmers into Farmer Interest Groups (FIGs), and onboard 300+ members (100+ in NE/Hilly areas).

2

Registration & Regulatory Compliance

Incorporate FPOs under the Companies Act or Cooperative Societies Act, draft by-laws, and secure Mandi, Seed, Fertilizer, Pesticide, FSSAI, and GST licenses.

3

Governance, BoD & Skill Building

Recruit FPO CEOs and accountants, train Board of Directors (including women directors), and deliver financial management & MIS training.

4

Business Planning & Infrastructure

Prepare 5-year viable business plans and facilitate access to warehouses, pack houses, NABL testing labs, grading equipment, and custom hiring centres.

5

Equity Grant & Finance Products

Facilitate Matching Equity Grants (up to ₹15 lakh/FPO) and Credit Guarantee Fund bank loans (up to ₹2 crore) via NABKISAN, NCDC, and commercial banks.

6

Market Insights & Linkages

Connect FPOs to e-NAM, ONDC Mystore, GeM, corporate buyers, buyer-seller meets, and export pathways for better price realization.
