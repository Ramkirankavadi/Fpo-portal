What Are Implementing Agencies & What Is Their Role?

Implementing Agencies (IAs) are statutory, cooperative, banking, academic, and state bodies entrusted by the Government of India to execute the 10,000 FPO Scheme through Cluster-Based Business Organizations (CBBOs) and Value Chain Organizations (VCOs).

1

CBBO Empanelment & Sanctioning

Select and engage qualified CBBOs/VCOs (494 CBBOs and 9 VCOs nationally; 144 CBBOs and 9 VCOs under SFAC alone) and assign geographic/commodity FPO clusters to them.

2

Fund Channelization & Milestones

Verify FPO incorporation, farmer onboarding, and business plans against defined milestones on the 10kfpomis.dac.gov.in portal before releasing FPO Management Cost (up to â‚¹18L) and CBBO Cost (up to â‚¹25L).

3

Equity Grant & Credit Linkage

4

Capacity Building & Market Access

Coordinate BoD/CEO training via BIRD Lucknow and LMS portals while connecting FPOs to e-NAM, ONDC, GeM, MSP procurement (NAFED/NCCF), and national FPO Melas.
