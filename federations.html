SFAC Federation Framework

What Is an FPO Federation & Why Does It Matter?

Supported by SFAC under the Companies Act as State-Level Producer Companies (SLPCs) or Cooperative Federations, federations serve as umbrella support systems owned by member FPOs.

1

Inter-FPO Coordination

Synchronizes commodity calendars, shared warehousing, and joint procurement across dozens or hundreds of primary FPOs within a state.

2

Collective Policy Dialogue

Represents smallholder farmer interests directly before State Agriculture Departments, Mandi Boards, and Central Ministries on APMC and tax reforms.

3

Access to Inputs & Technology

Negotiates manufacturer-level bulk rates for seeds, fertilizers, and farm machinery on behalf of all member FPOs.

4

Cost-Effective Capacity Building

Delivers standardized accounting, GST/FSSAI compliance, e-NAM/ONDC onboarding, and executive mentorship at scale.

5

Strengthened Creditworthiness

Aggregates balance sheets and Credit Guarantee Fund access to negotiate lower-interest working capital and trade finance from commercial banks.

6

Mega Agribusiness & Exports

Fulfills high-volume institutional tenders (NAFED, NCCF, FCI, GeM) and corporate/export contracts that individual FPOs cannot service alone.
